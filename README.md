# Helping Hand - CraftCulture Platform

An empowerment platform where handicraft artisans create and sell handmade products with profit sharing, access job opportunities suitable for craftspeople, and receive money/material donations. The system manages product listings, sales, profit distribution, job postings, and donation tracking for handicraft workers.

## Key Focus Areas
- Artisan product listing & sales
- Profit sharing mechanism
- Job opportunity management
- Money & material donation system
- Artisan & buyer dashboards

## Tech Stack
- Flutter
- E-Commerce
